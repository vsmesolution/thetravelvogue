Casa De Paradiso by Doghari Stays, Old Manali

by admin

A Place Where Valley Meets Mountain & You Will Meet Tranquility

Situated in Old Manali, this is an amazing property with 12 rooms with balcony facing east. It is a drive-in property with parking space. Rooms are available on First Floor, Second Floor & Third Floor. First Floor is cafeteria, lawn & reception. Fourth Floor is dedicated meditation area & is prohibited with permission.

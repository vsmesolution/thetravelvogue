Tour Overview and Highlights into Spiti Valley Road Trip 2025

The Spiti Valley circuit spans more than 1800 kilometers, taking you on a counterclockwise route from Delhi & Chandigarh through Shimla, Kinnaur, Spiti, Chandratal Lake Trek or hike and back via Manali. This carefully planned route ensures gradual acclimatization to high altitudes and maximizes your experience of diverse landscapes and cultural attractions.

Why Choose Our Spiti Valley Tour?

We’ve strategically designed this Spiti itinerary to follow the Delhi- Chandigarh-Shimla-Kinnaur-Kaza-Manali route for several compelling reasons:

1. Avoids summer traffic congestion on the Manali-Solang Valley-Atal Tunnel route
2. Approaches the challenging Gramphu-Batal road section from the easier Kaza side
3. Provides gradual altitude gain for better acclimatization, especially important for travelers from lower elevations
4. Offers a perfect blend of stunning landscapes, cultural immersion, and adventure

 

Detailed Day-by-Day Spiti Valley Itinerary

Day 1: Delhi to Narkanda (Late Night Departure)

Overnight Journey

Your Spiti Valley adventure begins with a late-night departure from Delhi.
We will drive overnight along National Highway 5, leaving the city’s hustle behind as we make our way toward the serene mountains.

It’s a relaxed journey under the stars, giving you time to rest and sleep.
By the time you wake up, you will be closer to the cool air and beautiful landscapes of Himachal Pradesh, setting the perfect tone for the days ahead.

Day 2: Arrive in Narkanda | Local Sightseeing and Hatu Peak Visit

Overnight Stay in Narkanda

We reach Narkanda early in the morning, just in time to witness the first light spreading across the Himalayan ranges.
After freshening up at the hotel or guest house, the morning is free for you to relax and soak in the peaceful mountain atmosphere.

Later, we head to the stunning Hatu Peak, the highest point around Narkanda.
The drive itself is beautiful, winding through thick forests of pine and deodar.
At the summit, enjoy panoramic 360-degree views of the snow-clad Himalayan ranges, stretching as far as the eye can see.

While there, you can also visit the quaint Hatu Mata Temple, a small wooden temple with deep-rooted legends and a serene setting that adds to the experience.

After returning, the rest of the day is yours to enjoy at leisure.
You can take a gentle stroll through nearby apple orchards, sip on a hot cup of tea with mountain views, or simply rest and acclimatize to the altitude.

Tonight, we stay in Narkanda, allowing you a slow, comfortable start to your grand Himalayan journey.

Bucket List Experiences in Narkanda:

  • Watch the magical sunrise over the Himalayan ranges

  • Drive through forest trails to Hatu Peak

  • Visit the ancient Hatu Mata Temple

  • Explore apple orchards and enjoy local mountain life

 

Upcoming Highlight of Spiti Valley Tour : Through Hindustand Tibet Highway , Enter Kinnaur District

Day 3: Narkanda to Sarahan to Sangla

Overnight Stay in Sangla

After a refreshing morning in Narkanda, we begin our scenic drive towards Sarahan.
Our first highlight is a visit to the famous Bhimakali Temple, a stunning example of traditional Himachali architecture with a history stretching back over 1,500 years.

From the temple complex, you’ll enjoy breathtaking views of Srikhand Mahadev Peak standing tall at 5,150 meters.
If time permits, we can also visit the nearby Western Tragopan Pheasantry, known for conserving one of the rarest pheasant species.

Continuing deeper into the mountains, we drive through the thrilling Tranda Dhank stretch — a historic cliffside road carved right into the mountain, offering dramatic views over the Sutlej River gorge.
It’s a drive where every turn is worth a photograph.

By late afternoon, we reach Sangla Valley, often called the hidden gem of Kinnaur, surrounded by apple orchards, pine forests, and snow-clad peaks.
Check into your hotel and enjoy a relaxed evening amidst the serene beauty of the valley.

Bucket List Experiences:

  • Explore the ancient Bhimakali Temple in Sarahan

  • Drive through the adventurous Tranda Dhank cliff road

  • Witness the rugged landscapes of Baspa Valley

  • Unwind in the peaceful surroundings of Sangla

Day 4: Sangla to Chitkul to Kalpa

Overnight Stay in Kalpa

After breakfast in Sangla, we set out for a short but stunning drive to Chitkul, the last inhabited village near the Indo-Tibet border, sitting proudly at 3,400 meters.
The charm of Chitkul lies in its traditional wooden houses, the sacred Mata Devi Temple, and mesmerizing views of the Baspa River flowing through the valley.

After spending a relaxed morning exploring Chitkul, we return to Sangla.
En route, we visit the Bering Nag Temple, one of the most revered temples in Kinnaur, and Kamru Fort, a historic structure showcasing centuries-old wood and stone architecture.

Post-lunch, we drive toward Kalpa, where a dramatic landscape of towering mountains and green valleys welcomes us.
Kalpa offers some of the best sunset and sunrise views over the mystical Kinnaur Kailash Range, a paradise for photography lovers and mountain dreamers.

Check into your hotel in Kalpa and spend the evening at leisure, breathing in the fresh mountain air and strolling through the quiet village streets.

Bucket List Experiences:

  • Visit Chitkul, the last village on the Indo-Tibet trade route

  • Explore the historic Kamru Fort and Bering Nag Temple

  • Capture stunning sunsets over the Kinnaur Kailash peaks

  • Walk through Kalpa’s traditional Himalayan villages and apple orchards

Upcoming Highlight of Spiti Valley Tour : Exiting Kinnaur & entering Spiti Region of Lahaul & Spiti District of Himachal Pradesh

Day 5: Kalpa to Tabo (Via Nako Lake, Khab Bridge & Geu Village)

Overnight Stay in Tabo

We start the day with a beautiful sunrise view over the mighty Kinnaur Kailash peaks. After breakfast, we leave Kalpa and begin our scenic drive deeper into Spiti Valley.

Our first major stop is Khab Bridge, where the roaring Sutlej and Spiti Rivers meet in a dramatic, cliff-lined gorge — a powerful sight you won’t forget.

From there, we continue toward Nako, a peaceful high-altitude village. Take a walk around Nako Lake, a serene spot surrounded by traditional stone houses and prayer flags fluttering in the wind.

Next, we visit Geu Village, a hidden gem famous for its 500-year-old naturally preserved mummy of a Buddhist monk — a rare and fascinating experience.

By evening, we reach Tabo, home to the oldest continuously operating monastery in India, founded in 996 AD.
Check into your hotel or guesthouse and rest up for the adventures ahead.

Bucket List Experiences:

  • Witness the dramatic confluence at Khab Bridge

  • Explore the beautiful Nako Lake and village

  • Visit the 500-year-old naturally preserved mummy at Geu Village

  • Experience the ancient history and spirituality of Tabo Monastery

Day 6: Tabo to Pin Valley | Mud Village | Dhankar Monastery | Kaza

Overnight Stay in Kaza

After breakfast, we set out toward the peaceful landscapes of Pin Valley, a less explored and truly pristine corner of Spiti.

Our main stop is Mud Village, the last motorable village in the valley. Surrounded by colorful fields and towering peaks, it’s the perfect place to slow down and soak in the raw beauty of the Himalayas.

Later, we visit the incredible Dhankar Monastery, perched dramatically on a rocky cliff.
The monastery offers sweeping views of the Spiti and Pin Rivers meeting far below — an unforgettable sight for photographers and travelers alike.

In the afternoon, we drive to Kaza, the lively administrative center of Spiti Valley.
Check in to your hotel, relax, and enjoy a calm evening in this high-altitude town.

Bucket List Experiences:

  • Explore the untouched beauty of Mud Village in Pin Valley

  • Visit the ancient Dhankar Monastery and admire its cliffside views

  • Capture breathtaking landscapes throughout the drive

  • Rest and recharge overnight in the heart of Spiti, Kaza

 

Ongoing Highlight in Spiti Valley Tour : Welcome to Real Spiti Valley Circuit : Where the Winds Whisper Mantras

Day 7: Kaza to Hikkim, Komic, Langza and Local Market Exploration

Overnight Stay in Kaza

Today promises to be one of the most exciting and memorable days of our Spiti journey, as we explore some of the highest inhabited places in the world.

After breakfast, we set out for a day of high-altitude village hopping. Our first stop is Hikkim, a small, charming village known for its incredible claim to fame — it houses the world’s highest post office.
Here, you will have a rare opportunity to send a postcard to your loved ones, stamped from this remote corner of the world. Imagine the joy when your friends or family receive a message that has traveled all the way from the Himalayas.

Next, we drive up to Komic, officially one of the world’s highest motorable villages. At around 4,513 meters, life here moves at a different rhythm, untouched by the rush of the cities. Visit the Tangyud Monastery, one of Spiti’s most significant monasteries, and soak in the peaceful energy that fills the village.

From Komic, we descend towards Langza, often called the “village guarded by Buddha.”
As you approach, you will be welcomed by the sight of a giant Buddha statue sitting atop a hill, watching over the entire valley. The landscapes here are wide open and surreal, offering fantastic opportunities for photography. Langza is also famous for its fossils — millions of years ago, this region was submerged under the Tethys Sea, and even today, locals sometimes find marine fossils embedded in the soil.

After exploring these picturesque villages, we return to Kaza by late afternoon.
The rest of the evening is free for you to wander through Kaza’s local market — the perfect place to shop for unique souvenirs, woolen clothing, handmade jewelry, Buddhist artifacts, and more. You can also relax at a cozy café, sampling some local dishes or enjoying a cup of warm butter tea.

It’s a relaxed, inspiring day that lets you experience the slower, more soulful side of life in the mountains.

Bucket List Experiences for Day 7:

  • Send a postcard from the world’s highest post office at Hikkim

  • Visit the ancient Tangyud Monastery in Komic

  • Marvel at the grand Buddha statue overlooking Langza village

  • Explore Langza’s fossil-rich lands and surreal landscapes

  • Shop for local souvenirs, woolens, and crafts in Kaza’s vibrant market

Things to Carry for High-Altitude Village Visits (Hikkim, Komic, Langza)

Exploring the world’s highest villages is an incredible experience, but the high altitude and strong mountain sun mean a little preparation goes a long way.
Here’s a simple checklist to help you stay comfortable during the day:

1. Warm Clothing:
Even if the sun is out, it can get windy and chilly at high altitudes.

  • Carry a light jacket or a windcheater

  • Wear layers so you can adjust easily

2. Sun Protection:
The sun is strong at 4,000+ meters.

  • Sunglasses with UV protection

  • A good quality sunscreen (SPF 50+)

  • A cap or hat to protect from direct sun

3. Water and Snacks:

  • Carry a reusable water bottle (stay hydrated)

  • Light snacks like energy bars, dry fruits, or chocolates

4. Personal Essentials:

  • Lip balm and moisturizer (the air is dry)

  • Personal medicines if you have any altitude sensitivity

  • A small day backpack to keep your hands free while exploring

5. Camera/Phone:
The landscapes are surreal — you’ll definitely want to capture memories. Make sure your phone/camera is charged, and maybe keep a small power bank handy.

6. Easy Footwear:
Wear comfortable walking shoes — the villages are best explored on foot over uneven paths.

Important Tip:
Walk slowly, breathe deeply, and enjoy the silence. The beauty of these places is not just in their landscapes, but in their peaceful rhythm of life.

Upcoming Highlight of Spiti Valley Tour : Beautiful Walk to Chandratal Lake Trek Experience Time

Day 8: Kaza to Chandratal via Key Monastery and Chicham Bridge

Overnight Stay at Chandratal Camp

Today is one of the most beautiful and exciting days of our journey, as we leave behind the town of Kaza and head deeper into the magic of Spiti’s highlands.

After breakfast, we begin the day with a visit to Key Monastery, Spiti’s largest and most iconic monastery, perched dramatically on a hilltop overlooking the valley. As we step inside, you’ll immediately feel the peaceful energy of this ancient place. Explore the beautifully decorated prayer halls, the old murals, and if we’re lucky, catch a glimpse of the monks going about their daily rituals. Don’t miss the panoramic view from the monastery terrace — the endless Spiti Valley stretching out below is a sight to remember.

From Key, we continue our journey toward Chicham Bridge. This is not just any bridge — it’s one of Asia’s highest suspension bridges, hanging 4,145 meters above sea level. As we drive across, you’ll see deep gorges beneath and endless mountain landscapes around — a thrilling yet stunning experience that’s a favorite for photography enthusiasts.

After crossing Chicham, we make our way toward the Kunzum Pass (subject to weather conditions). Standing at around 4,551 meters, Kunzum offers breathtaking views of snow-capped peaks and colorful prayer flags fluttering in the strong mountain winds. It’s a traditional spot where travelers often stop to seek blessings for a safe onward journey.

From Kunzum, we descend toward one of Spiti’s crown jewels — Chandratal Lake, lovingly known as the “Moon Lake” because of its crescent shape.
Vehicles stop a short distance away, and from there, it’s an easy 10-15 minute walk to the lake. As you approach, the crystal-clear waters reflecting the surrounding mountains create a surreal sight — it almost feels like walking into a painting.

The evening is yours to relax by the lake, soaking in the changing hues as the sun dips behind the peaks. This is truly one of those places where time seems to stand still. Later, we head back to our cozy campsite near Chandratal.
Tonight, you’ll experience something unforgettable — camping under a sky full of a million stars, surrounded by the silence of the high Himalayas.

A magical end to a day that captures the true spirit of Spiti.

Camping Tips for Chandratal Lake

Spending a night near Chandratal is truly a once-in-a-lifetime experience — but keep in mind, you are camping at over 4,200 meters in the Himalayas.
Here’s what to know so you are comfortable and can fully enjoy this magical night:

Stay Comfort:
You will be staying in comfortable Swiss tents, complete with cozy bedding and attached bathrooms — a rare luxury at such high altitudes. The tents are weather-proof and provide good insulation against the nighttime cold.

Weather:
Nights at Chandratal can get quite chilly, even in peak summer (temperatures can drop close to 0°C or below).
Carry a good quality jacket, warm thermals, and woolen socks to stay cozy inside the tent.

Essential Carry Items:

  • Warm layers (thermals, jackets, gloves, beanies)

  • Torch or headlamp (even though camps are well-lit)

  • Personal toiletries

  • Power bank (electricity backup can be limited)

  • Reusable water bottle (to stay hydrated)

  • Some snacks (energy bars, chocolates for late night cravings)

Health Tip:
Due to the high altitude, it is normal to feel a bit short of breath initially. Walk slowly, stay hydrated, and avoid heavy exertion after sunset.
We also ensure basic first-aid and oxygen support is available at the campsite if needed.

Experience Tip:
Step outside your tent after dinner for stargazing — Chandratal is one of the best places for a natural stargazing experience. With no city lights around, the sky lights up with millions of stars, offering an unforgettable night under the Himalayan sky.

Bucket List Experiences for Day 8:

  • Visit the historic Key Monastery, the heart of Spiti’s spiritual life

  • Drive across the thrilling Chicham Bridge

  • Cross the majestic Kunzum Pass (weather permitting)

  • Short hike to the stunning Chandratal Lake

  • Stargazing and overnight camping near Chandratal

 

Upcoming Highlight of Spiti Valley Tour : Exiting Lahaul Spiti Valley & entering Kullu Valley of Himachal Pradesh

Day 9: Chandratal to Manali via Sissu and Solang Valley

Overnight Stay in Manali

After a peaceful night under the starry skies at Chandratal, we start our journey back towards Manali after breakfast. But today’s drive is much more than just getting from one point to another — it’s a visual treat every step of the way.

As we cross over the Kunzum Pass, you’ll catch your last glimpses of the raw, untouched beauty of Spiti Valley. Slowly, the landscape changes as we enter the Lahaul Valley — a lush, green contrast to Spiti’s barren charm.

Our first highlight today is a stop at Sissu, a picture-perfect Himalayan village straight out of a postcard. Nestled along the banks of the Chandra River, Sissu is known for its cascading waterfalls, vast meadows, and peaceful vibes. It’s a great place to stretch your legs, breathe in the fresh mountain air, and grab a few beautiful photographs.

From Sissu, we continue toward Solang Valley, one of Himachal’s most popular adventure hubs. Depending on the weather and how everyone’s feeling, you can opt for a range of adventure activities here:

  • Zip-lining across deep valleys

  • Paragliding for a bird’s-eye view of the Himalayas

  • ATV rides across rugged mountain trails

  • Cable car rides offering panoramic views of Solang and beyond

Even if you’re not up for adventure sports, Solang Valley is a fantastic place to simply relax, sip a cup of hot tea, and take in the magnificent scenery all around.

After soaking up the thrills or the views, we head toward Manali. By evening, we’ll reach this vibrant mountain town, where you’ll check into a cozy hotel. The rest of the evening is yours — relax at the property, explore Manali’s bustling streets, or maybe visit the famous Mall Road for some local shopping and delicious food.

It’s a day filled with changing landscapes, optional adventures, and moments you’ll want to capture both on camera and in your memory.

Bucket List Experiences on Day 9:

  • Cross from the rugged terrains of Spiti into the lush valleys of Kullu

  • Explore the charming village of Sissu and its waterfalls

  • Enjoy optional adventure sports in Solang Valley

  • Relax and unwind with a comfortable overnight stay in Manali

 

Day 10: Manali to Delhi/Chandigarh | Day Journey with Scenic Enroute Exploration

After a hearty breakfast in Manali, we begin our final drive back to Delhi or Chandigarh. But this isn’t just a travel day — today’s journey is packed with beautiful landscapes, charming towns, and opportunities for a few last adventures in the lap of the Himalayas.

We first drive through the Kullu Valley, known for its apple orchards, pine forests, and the Beas River flowing alongside the road. As we descend, we pass through Kullu town, famous for its colorful Dussehra festival and traditional handwoven shawls. If time permits, you can explore the local Kullu market for some authentic souvenirs.

Further down, we reach Mandi, often called the “Varanasi of the Hills” because of its 80+ old stone temples. The town sits beautifully on the banks of the Beas River, offering a glimpse into Himachal’s rich heritage. En route, we can stop for a quick tea break or stretch our legs while soaking in the historic vibe.

Continuing along the scenic highway, we travel through Sundernagar, famous for its large artificial lake and beautiful views. It’s a perfect shortstop to admire the stillness of the water against the backdrop of hills.

Adventure lovers, get ready — somewhere between Mandi and Bilaspur, depending on the group’s interest and time, we can also stop for water adventure activities like:

  • River Rafting in the Beas River (near Pandoh and Aut regions)

  • Boating at Gobind Sagar Lake in Bilaspur (subject to weather conditions)

As we move forward, the landscape changes — from rugged mountains to gentle hills, and eventually to the plains. We drive past Bilaspur, known for the huge Gobind Sagar reservoir created by the Bhakra Dam, and finally cross into the plains of Punjab/Haryana by evening.

Expect multiple scenic stops along the way for refreshments, photography breaks, and local food experiences.
We aim to reach Delhi or Chandigarh by late evening, depending on traffic and the final drop-off point.

Today is a day to relax, share stories, relive your favorite moments from the trip, and soak in the last few hours of your Spiti road trip adventure. It’s the perfect way to end a journey that started with excitement and leaves you with a lifetime of memories.

Bucket List Experiences on Day 10:

  • Drive through the beautiful Kullu Valley

  • Shop for souvenirs in Kullu market

  • Explore the temple town of Mandi

  • Optional river rafting or boating adventures

  • Scenic drive along Gobind Sagar Lake

  • End your epic Spiti road trip with heartfelt memories
